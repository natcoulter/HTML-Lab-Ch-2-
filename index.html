Sacramento, California (surprise surprise it's Cali)

「 ✦ Location ✦ 」

Sacramento is the capital of California, nestled in the Central Valley, and home to a historic district rich in history.

「 ✦ About ✦ 」

Historic District

Sacramento's historic district is filled with old architecture and historical sights, dating back to the gold rush.

Favorite Spots:

  • California State Railroad Museum
  • Evangeline's Costume Shop
  • The Candy Barrel

Downtown

Sacramento's downtown area has some beautiful spots and businesses. There are many restaurants that serve amazing food, and the rose garden is beautiful when in full bloom.

Events

Many large events are hosted at either the SAFE Credit Union Convention Center or CAL Expo, with the event types varying from anime to business conventions.

「 ✦ Things to Do ✦ 」

  • Visiting the museums scattered in both the historic districts and downtown districts (the Crocker Art Museum is a good recommendation)
  • Check out the food scene. If you enjoy ramen, Ryujin Ramen House on S st. in Downtown is delicious and worth a visit if you're ever in the area!
  • Visit the mall. It was one of the biggest places to hang out at growing up.
  • SacAnime Convention
